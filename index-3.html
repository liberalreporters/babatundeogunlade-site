About Babatunde Sunday Ogunlade

A professional background. A community-centred outlook.

Hon. Babatunde Sunday Ogunlade is an entrepreneur and professional whose experience spans business, administration and community engagement.

His professional background includes accounting and business administration, with experience associated with Skylark Homes & Properties.

His public leadership proposition is centred on practical representation, economic opportunity, infrastructure development and stronger connections between constituency needs and legislative action.

Development Priorities

Moving Ibarapa East Forward.

The proposed priorities focus on areas where legislative representation can connect public resources, private partnerships and community needs.

Priority 01

Agriculture & Market Access

Supporting agricultural productivity, access to equipment and inputs, stronger market connections and improved feeder-road infrastructure.

Priority 02

Infrastructure & Connectivity

Advocating for improved roads, electricity, connectivity and infrastructure that support households, businesses and communities.

Priority 03

Skills, Enterprise & Technology

Promoting vocational development, technology training, entrepreneurship and private-sector pathways for young people.

Priority 04

Community Representation

Building stronger channels for residents, community organisations and stakeholders to communicate constituency priorities.

Policy note: These priorities represent proposed areas of focus and should be read as a public leadership agenda rather than a guarantee of specific legislative outcomes.
