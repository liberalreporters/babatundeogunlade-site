The Qualifications

Leadership You Can Trust.

​

Tested Professional & Expert

Bringing years of administrative experience and financial expertise from the private sector to ensure efficient governance and public accountability.

​

Grassroots Community Builder

An accessible representative deeply connected to the unique fabric of Ibarapa East communities across Lanlate, Eruwa, and surrounding towns.

​

Development-Oriented Vision

Focused on actionable legislative frameworks that prioritize local agricultural growth, infrastructure, and youth empowerment.

​
Proposed Manifesto

Legislative Agenda for 2027

Structured legislative pillars designed for verifiable progress in Ibarapa East.

​
Primary Focus

Agricultural Revitalization & Market Access

Attracting state and private partnerships for modern farming equipment, seedling distribution, and the rehabilitation of critical feeder roads connecting farmers to major urban markets.

PILLAR I
​
Infrastructure

Rural Electrification & Roads

Ensuring Ibarapa East receives its fair share of state infrastructure budgets, focusing heavily on stable electricity supply and intra-city connectivity.

PILLAR II
​
Youth & Enterprise

Ibarapa Skills & Tech Hub

Driving legislation to establish localized vocational and tech training centers to combat youth unemployment through structured private sector models.

Private Sector Success
​

Experience that understands our people.

Before entering public service, Hon. Babatunde Ogunlade built a solid reputation in business administration. His leadership at Skylark demonstrates a proven ability to manage complex projects, allocate resources efficiently, and execute sustainable long-term strategies.

These are the exact managerial competencies required in the Oyo State House of Assembly to ensure optimal constituency representation and tangible dividends of democracy.
